{# ── 1. Like-minded countries as a positive force (anchor kept as #vertrekpunt) ── #}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 15 #}{{ _('Like-minded countries as a positive force') }}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 1 #}{% trans %}Major powers pay too little attention to international law. The Global Union (GU) offers an alternative: a permanent cooperation between functioning democracies, as a complement to the EU and the UN. Together we want to use our market power for prosperity, social security, a liveable planet, security and the enforcement of international agreements.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 2 #}{% trans %}Any country that scores well on democracy, the rule of law, human rights and anti-corruption can join. A solid foundation already exists. In October 2026, the EU and Canada formed an alliance for the future. Like-minded countries have been invited to join. Together, our economy is more than one third of the world's and larger than that of the US.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 3 #}{% trans %}The GU is principled and pragmatic. Principled, because trade tariffs depend on how a country scores on honouring international agreements and shared values. Countries that meet all criteria pay no import duties. Countries that commit aggression are excluded. Pragmatic, because we start small, with limited tariffs. We want to be attractive and avoid unnecessary escalation. This gives us time to become strategically independent together.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 4 #}{% trans %}Those who join benefit. That gives countries a reason to reform, as happened earlier with the EU. This is how the union grows: through attraction.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 16 #}{{ _('Core principles of the Global Union') }}

01

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 17 #}{{ _('A values-driven trade system') }}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 18 #}{{ _('The GU operates with a single system of trade tariffs based on a scorecard per country, measured on:') }}

  • {# TRANSLATORS: manifest, lijstitem 5 #}{% trans %}Anti-corruption{% endtrans %}
  • {# TRANSLATORS: manifest, lijstitem 6 #}{% trans %}Freedom: rule of law, democracy, free press{% endtrans %}
  • {# TRANSLATORS: manifest, lijstitem 7 #}{% trans %}Human rights{% endtrans %}
  • {# TRANSLATORS: manifest, lijstitem 8 #}{% trans %}Sustainability: climate accords, respect for nature{% endtrans %}
  • {# TRANSLATORS: manifest, lijstitem 9 #}{% trans %}Conduct within the international legal order: IP protection, no espionage, no dumping, contribution to the International Court of Justice{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 10 #}{{ _('Countries that fully comply pay 0% import duties. Countries that partially comply pay based on their score, with yearly measurement and adjustment. Countries that commit aggression or murder their own people are fully excluded, and countries that continue to trade with them face higher tariffs.') }}

02

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 19 #}{{ _('Peaceful but firm foreign policy') }}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 11 #}{% trans %}The GU invests in strategic independence for all members: raw materials, energy, defence and its own AI capabilities. The GU must urgently stand on its own two feet in these areas.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 12 #}{% trans %}The GU does not pursue assertive military action, unless a broad majority of GU members judges intervention necessary because of crimes against a country's own population. Democratisation in autocratic countries happens through uprising from within the population itself. If regimes misbehave, we support the opposition.{% endtrans %}

03

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 20 #}{{ _('Information freedom as a strategic instrument') }}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 21 #}{{ _('The GU extends its influence through high-quality independent journalism in all local languages - by analogy to the BBC, reinforced with GU resources - and by making protected technology available that frees people from state-controlled internet access.') }}

04

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 22 #}{{ _('A single trade zone, limited free movement of people') }}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 13 #}{% trans %}The GU does not function as a single market with full free movement of people, to avoid unwanted migration flows toward wealthier areas. Uniform trade tariffs based on the scorecard do apply.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 14 #}{% trans %}For high-value work, we issue temporary work visas to scarce talent from GU countries as well as from non-GU countries. The principle is circular: those who work abroad temporarily bring knowledge, experience and networks back to their home country.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 15 #}{% trans %}The GU also invests in training programmes through which people in non-GU countries can develop to the level needed to make a valuable contribution. Once back home, they can continue to contribute to the GU economy remotely. This way prosperity grows on both sides.{% endtrans %}

{# ── 3. Landen ──────────────────────────────────── #}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 23 #}{{ _('Countries') }}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 16 #}{% trans %}Accession is tied to three existing, independent indices: the Democracy Index, the Corruption Perceptions Index, and the Human Rights Index. For each index, a 'sufficient' level and a 'minimum' level have been chosen. The minimum level is chosen so that the EU can join as a bloc.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 17 #}{% trans %}Countries above the minimum level are invited to join.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 18 #}{% trans %}Three countries receive separate treatment. The United States and Israel are not invited for now. These countries do numerically meet the standard, but it cannot be expected that, under their current administrations, they would want to commit to the rules of the Global Union. Taiwan also qualifies, but lacks UN-recognised status. For this we can work with a separate status, as is already the case at the WTO.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 19 #}{% trans %}The indices carry a limited margin of error. Countries that fall just short of the threshold can still be assessed on request.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 33 #}{% trans %}The Global Union does not apply a single external tariff, but different tariffs per country, based on the score on the values scorecard. This appears to conflict with the World Trade Organization's principle that all countries should receive equal tariffs. Under Article XX GATT, protection of public morals, we expect to be able to make an exception here. Because this judgement is carried by the whole Global Union, and applied using fixed criteria equal for every country, this claim stands on firmer ground than if a single country made it alone.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 34 #}{% trans %}We do not adopt agricultural policy, cohesion funds, or the monetary union; these are inseparably tied to the EU's own territory and budget.{% endtrans %}

{# ── 6. Organisatie ─────────────────────────────── #}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, tekst 34 #}{{ _('Organisation') }}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 76 #}{% trans %}The Global Union operates with a minimal organisation: a small commission and a limited civil service, supported by existing national and international institutions. The budget stays limited, since the union starts with a limited set of competences and does not take on large transfers such as agricultural or cohesion funds. Yet the union is effective: decisions are taken by a clear majority rather than unanimity. Implementation takes place within the countries themselves. Oversight of that implementation is carried out by the Global Union. There is, however, budget for international student exchange and for independent online news media and education programmes.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: manifest, alinea 77 #}{% trans %}Discussions and votes take place online. This effectiveness is strengthened by democratic innovation. Decision-making is transparent, including the underlying facts and considerations, explained at both expert and layperson level. Citizens worldwide can take part and exert influence in their own language.{% endtrans %}
