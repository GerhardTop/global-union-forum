Toetreding is gekoppeld aan drie bestaande, onafhankelijke indices: de Democracy Index, de Corruption Perceptions Index en de Human Rights Index. De niveaus zijn arbitrair gekozen, maar bewust zo dat de EU als blok kan toetreden. Landen die net onder de drempel zitten, kunnen op aanvraag alsnog worden beoordeeld.

Drie landen krijgen een aparte behandeling. Accession is tied to three existing, independent indices: the Democracy Index, the Corruption Perceptions Index, and the Human Rights Index. The levels are arbitrarily chosen, but deliberately so that the EU can join as a bloc. Countries close to the threshold can still be assessed on request.

Three countries receive separate treatment. The United States and Israel numerically meet the standard, but accession is not on the table for now: a genuine commitment to our values cannot be expected under the current administrations. Taiwan also qualifies, but lacks UN-recognised status; for this we can work with a separate status, as is already the case at the WTO.
