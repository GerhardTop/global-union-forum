Gerhard Top

{# TRANSLATORS: initiatiefnemer, tekst 3 #}{{ _('Initiator · Global Union Forum') }}

{# TRANSLATORS: initiatiefnemer, alinea 1 #}{% trans %}At the end of my student years the Wall fell. At the moment of the breakthrough I was working on a Land Rover. Over the radio I heard the jubilant East Germans, and then a German version of a song by the Dutch singer Boudewijn de Groot: 'Nur die Vögel fliegen von Ost nach West Berlin...'. Goosebumps! Unexpected, unimaginable, that dictatorship simply collapsed.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: initiatiefnemer, alinea 2 #}{% trans %}With that Land Rover the four of us drove to Cape Town. Straight through Africa. I saw the beauty and the poverty. The cheerfulness and the enormous corruption and political repression.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: initiatiefnemer, alinea 3 #}{% trans %}Ten years after that Africa trip I took part in a meeting of the D66 Business Club. We discussed the misuse of development funds, the difficulties for businesses that refused to participate in corruption, clientelism and how deeply it was embedded in the culture. The atmosphere turned somewhat hopeless, but no one had a solution.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: initiatiefnemer, alinea 4 #}{% trans %}This spring I attended a member meeting of Transparency International. The all-corrupting rot is still widespread in Africa and in many other countries. This lack of development, and the negative trend in the growing number of countries that undermine the rule of law, including the United States, means that a counterforce is needed. A powerful cooperation between functioning democracies, a geopolitical force that can also serve as a magnet for other countries. With the Global Union Forum I want to make a contribution by facilitating a meaningful conversation, enriching the idea along the way, and broadening support for it.{% endtrans %}

{# TRANSLATORS: initiatiefnemer, alinea 5 #}{% trans %}Since Covid I have been a lecturer at the University of Applied Sciences in Amsterdam. After years in leadership roles in software and telecom I wanted a more socially engaged role. I succeeded, I work with students from all kinds of backgrounds, including refugees. Sometimes I have conversations with them that move me. They make me want more and more countries to be places where young people grow up in freedom, with prospects for growth in prosperity and wellbeing.{% endtrans %}
